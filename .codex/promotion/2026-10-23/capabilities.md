## Publication language

Use “enabled by default” for Memory. Explain retrieval, provenance and freshness without an experimental qualifier. Never say unlimited context, lossless compression, flawless/unattended success, absolute reliability, independent-context security isolation, complete A2A Server, or originator of Harness. No chronological priority claim is needed for this campaign.

Private source paths support this internal audit; external drafts cite public documentation and inspectable screenshots instead of reproducing proprietary code.
